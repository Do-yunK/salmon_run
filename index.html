Salmon is abundance: a life given so that families and communities may live.

The salmon return home through the rapids, feeding the river, the forest and the bears. Move the net with your mouse and click to scoop a salmon.

Each salmon you take feeds you, and what comes next is your choice. Give thanks and the run is sustained. Take without gratitude and the river slowly empties. Pause, reflect, and choose.

Salmon is a symbol of abundance, prosperity and the cycle of life: a foundational food that sustains families and communities, giving its life so others may live.

Inspired by the reciprocal relationship many Indigenous peoples of Canada hold with salmon, bears and the rivers that sustain them.
